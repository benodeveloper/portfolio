ABRID Connect is the public face of the Souss-Massa entrepreneurship ecosystem. It brings together
entrepreneurs, startups, freelancers and cooperatives on one side, and the organizations that can
help them on the other: support programs, experts and financing.

I built it at go-digital for CRI Souss-Massa, alongside the institution's support-request platform.
