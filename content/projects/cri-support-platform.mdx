As the primary architect, I designed the CRI Souss-Massa investor and project support platform from
the data model to deployment.

## What it does

- Project holders and investors submit support requests and are guided through the process.
- An advanced business plan generator produces investor business plan models, with notices in
  French and Arabic.

## Stack

Laravel 11, React and Inertia.js, with Reverb for real-time updates, Redis, and Docker.
