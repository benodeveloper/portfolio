At Grafimage I managed and scaled all-souss-massa, the regional tourism platform for CRT Agadir,
built on Laravel 6.

The main challenge was traffic: the platform sustained peaks above 80K visitors after I tuned Apache,
PHP-FPM sockets and the database queries behind the busiest pages.
