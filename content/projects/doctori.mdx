I worked on Doctori as a full-stack developer from February 2022 to October 2024, in an 8-person team
of three developers, two UI/UX designers, two videographers and one growth marketer.

## Highlights

- Helped lead the refactor of the core backend monolith from Symfony to Laravel.
- Built real-time online video consultations with the Vonage Video API.
- Developed the cross-platform mobile app in Flutter, for a platform serving 6.2K+ monthly visitors.
